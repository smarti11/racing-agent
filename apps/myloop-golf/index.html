Two apps. One club brand. Built for the phone golfers already carry.

Course App

Everyday GPS and scoring, under your name.

Members open a club-branded map — not a consumer app full of ads — and play with live yardages, green reads, and a scorecard that stays on their phone.

  • Live GPS yardages Front, middle, and back of the green from the golfer’s location, with plays-like adjustments for uphill and downhill.
  • Rings, flyovers, and breaks 100 / 150 / 200 yard curves, hole flyover video, and green slope arrows with a putt read you can drag.
  • Personal scorecard Enter strokes by tap or voice. Save the round to Files or Notes when the round is done.
  • No cart hardware Works on the phones already in the bag. Your brand, your courses, your tees and handicaps.

Outing App

Tournament day without the scramble of paper.

Shotguns, member-guests, and classics get a phone experience for the event — GPS on the course that day, group scoring, and sponsor presence on every hole.

  • Event GPS Only the course you’re playing that day, with live distances while groups are on the hole.
  • One scorekeeper per group Scramble and outing formats where one phone enters the score and the rest stay view-only.
  • Hole sponsors Each hole can carry the sponsor takeover for the outing — built for classics and charity days.
  • Silent auction browse Players can flip through auction items on the phone during the day (bidding stays at the desk).
