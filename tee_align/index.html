Tee Align Marker square-up
AIM — fairway target

Set tee markers square

  1. Stand behind the teeing ground, looking down the hole.
  2. Put the vertical AIM line on the fairway spot you want the tee to face.
  3. Drag the on-screen L and R markers along the horizontal line to the spacing you want (Equal lock keeps them mirrored).
  4. Place the physical tee markers to match those on-screen handles.

Works best in Safari on iPhone. Use the rear camera outdoors; pick a line color that shows against grass or sky.
