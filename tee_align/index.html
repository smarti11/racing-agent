Set tee markers square

  1. Stand behind the teeing ground, looking down the hole.
  2. Watch the level bubble — keep the phone steady and mostly upright.
  3. Put AIM on the fairway target. Use Snap level to square the tee-face to gravity, or Rotate / two-finger twist.
  4. Drag L/R for spacing (Equal lock mirrors). Check the spacing badge.
  5. Tap Freeze before walking out to place markers. Save photo keeps a record. Sun mode boosts outdoor contrast.

Works best in Safari on iPhone over HTTPS. Use the rear camera outdoors.
