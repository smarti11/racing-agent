POPLAR CORNER SHELVES — SHOPPING LIST
====================================

WOOD
----
[ ] Poplar — 2 shelves @ 20 × 6 × 1.5 in (+25% waste)
[ ] Poplar (or paint-grade) for cleats — four pieces (see cut-list.txt)
    Prefer: 1×6×8 ft poplar for shelves+cleats, or 8/4 shelves + 1×2 cleats

HARDWARE
--------
[ ] Structural screws into studs for cleats (#8 or #10, 2.5–3 in typical)
[ ] Finish screws or pin nails to fasten shelves down onto cleats
[ ] Wood glue (Titebond II or III) — if laminating shelves
[ ] Optional: L-brackets in the inside corner under each shelf for extra stiffness
[ ] Wall anchors ONLY if a cleat screw cannot hit a stud (prefer studs)

FINISH
------
[ ] Sandpaper: 120, 180, 220 grit
[ ] Waterborne polyurethane (preferred) OR oil + poly
[ ] Paint or finish for cleats (wall-match paint hides them)
[ ] Tack cloth / microfiber rags

TOOLS
-----
[ ] Stud finder
[ ] 24 in level
[ ] Tape measure, pencil, painter’s tape
[ ] Drill / driver
[ ] Saw for square cuts; clamps if laminating
[ ] Optional: pocket-hole jig if joining L-brackets/cleat returns
