WALL MARKING WORKSHEET — Corner Mount (Left + Back)
===================================================
Shelves attach to the LEFT wall and the BACK (rear) wall.
Use painter’s tape. Keep lines plumb and level.

STEP 1 — Find the corner
------------------------
1. Mark the inside corner where left wall meets rear wall.
2. Run a vertical plumb tape strip up from the tank area along that corner.
   This is CORNER REFERENCE (not tank centerline).

STEP 2 — Bottom shelf height
----------------------------
Clearance above tank lid (default): 26 in (range 24–28)
Basket on tank must clear the underside of the lower shelf.

Mark LOWER SHELF BOTTOM at: tank lid + ______ in (default 26)
LOWER SHELF TOP = bottom + 1.5 in

STEP 3 — Upper shelf height
---------------------------
Clear gap (default): 12 in
UPPER SHELF BOTTOM = lower shelf top + 12 in
UPPER SHELF TOP = upper shelf bottom + 1.5 in

Defaults:
  Lower bottom:  +26.0 in above lid
  Lower top:     +27.5 in above lid
  Upper bottom:  +39.5 in above lid
  Upper top:     +41.0 in above lid

STEP 4 — Shelf footprint on both walls
--------------------------------------
Along REAR wall from corner: mark 0 in (left end) and 20 in (right end).
Along LEFT wall from corner: mark 0 in (back) and 6 in (front edge of shelf).

Confirm the 20 in right end clears the towel cabinet by ≥ 1 in. If not, shorten
length and recut (e.g. 18 in).

STEP 5 — Studs on BOTH walls
----------------------------
Rear-wall studs across 0–20 in from corner: ______ in, ______ in, ______ in
Left-wall studs across 0–6 in from corner:  ______ in, ______ in

Every cleat needs at least one solid stud hit; prefer two on the longer back cleat.

STEP 6 — Cleat outlines
-----------------------
For each shelf elevation, tape the back-wall cleat outline (level) and the
left-wall cleat outline (level, same elevation). Tops of both cleats must align
so the shelf sits flat in the corner.

CHECKLIST
---------
[ ] Corner plumb reference marked
[ ] Lower and upper elevations marked on BOTH walls
[ ] 20 in / 6 in footprint confirmed clear of cabinet
[ ] Studs mapped on left wall and rear wall
[ ] Cleat outlines level and coplanar
