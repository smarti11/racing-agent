WALL MARKING WORKSHEET — Centered Over Toilet
==============================================
Use painter’s tape on the wall. Do not rely on pencil alone for long vertical lines.

STEP 1 — Tank centerline
------------------------
1. Measure toilet tank lid width: ______ in
2. Half-width: ______ in
3. From left edge of tank lid, mark center on the wall.
4. Run a vertical strip of painter’s tape from tank lid up past the upper shelf zone.
   This is CENTERLINE. Both shelves share it.

STEP 2 — Bottom shelf height
----------------------------
Clearance above tank lid (plan default): 26 in (range 24–28 in)
Basket / items on tank must clear the underside of the lower shelf.

Mark LOWER SHELF BOTTOM at: tank lid + ______ in (default 26)
Then mark LOWER SHELF TOP = bottom + 1.5 in shelf thickness.

STEP 3 — Upper shelf height
---------------------------
Clear gap between shelves (plan default): 12 in clear
UPPER SHELF BOTTOM = lower shelf top + 12 in
UPPER SHELF TOP = upper shelf bottom + 1.5 in

Computed (with defaults):
  Lower bottom:  +26.0 in above lid
  Lower top:     +27.5 in above lid
  Upper bottom:  +39.5 in above lid
  Upper top:     +41.0 in above lid

STEP 4 — Shelf length / side marks
----------------------------------
Shelf length: 20 in → 10 in each side of centerline.

On each shelf bottom line, mark:
  LEFT end  = centerline − 10 in
  RIGHT end = centerline + 10 in

Confirm right end clears the towel cabinet by ≥ 1 in. If not, shorten length
symmetrically (e.g. 18 in → ±9 in from center).

STEP 5 — Studs
--------------
Scan and mark every stud across the 20 in shelf span (and ~2 in beyond each end).

Stud locations relative to centerline (negative = left):
  ______ in, ______ in, ______ in, ______ in

If studs are not symmetric under the centered shelf, use a plate-style / ledger-style
floating bracket so fasteners hit studs while the shelf remains visually centered.

STEP 6 — Bracket plate outline
------------------------------
Trace each bracket backplate on the wall, level, with the shelf bottom line as reference
per kit instructions. Recheck level before driving screws.
