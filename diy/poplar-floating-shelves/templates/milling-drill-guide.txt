MILLING AND BRACKET DRILL GUIDE
===============================
Target finished blank (each): 20.00 × 6.00 × 1.50 in — qty 2

1. Laminate (if using 3/4 in stock)
-----------------------------------
- Joint mating faces, apply glue evenly, clamp every 6–8 in.
- Align edges flush on the back (wall) side.
- Cure per glue instructions (≥ 1 hour clamp; overnight before machining).

2. Mill to final size
---------------------
- Plane or sand to 1.50 in thick.
- Rip to 6.00 in depth.
- Crosscut both ends square to 20.00 in length.
- Ease all exposed edges (hand break or 1/8 in roundover). Leave back top/bottom
  edges relatively sharp so the shelf sits tight to the wall.

3. Mark identical blanks
------------------------
- Label backs: SHELF A / SHELF B, and mark UP / WALL.
- Transfer centerline on the back edge of each shelf (at 10 in).

4. Drill for floating brackets
------------------------------
- Follow kit template exactly for rod diameter and depth.
- Typical pattern: two rods, inset several inches from each end, centered on
  shelf thickness, bored from the back face.
- Use a drill press if available; otherwise a guide block to keep holes square.
- Practice on scrap first (see cut-list scrap piece).
- Dry-fit rods — shelf must slide on fully and sit flush to a flat reference wall.

5. Pre-finish check
-------------------
[ ] Both shelves identical within 1/32 in
[ ] Bracket rods fully seat
[ ] No tear-out on visible faces
[ ] Back face flat
