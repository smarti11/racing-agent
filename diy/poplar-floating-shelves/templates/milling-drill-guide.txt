MILLING GUIDE — Corner Shelves + Cleats
=======================================
Target shelf (each): 20.00 × 6.00 × 1.50 in — qty 2
Cleats: 2 back-wall + 2 left-wall (see cut-list.txt)

1. Laminate shelves (if using 3/4 in stock)
------------------------------------------
- Glue, clamp, cure overnight before final milling.

2. Mill shelves to final size
-----------------------------
- Plane/sand to 1.50 in thick; rip to 6.00 in; crosscut to 20.00 in.
- LEFT end: dead square and crisp (mates to left wall).
- BACK edge: dead square and crisp (mates to rear wall).
- Ease FRONT edge and RIGHT end only (~1/16 in break or 1/8 in roundover).

3. Label orientation
--------------------
- Mark each shelf: LEFT END, BACK EDGE, UP, FRONT.
- Dry-fit in the actual corner before finishing — check for wall out-of-square.
  If walls are out of square, scribe the back/left edges to the wall lines.

4. Mill cleats
--------------
- Back cleats: ~18.5 × 1.5 × 0.75 in (or full 20 in if preferred).
- Left cleats: ~5.0 × 1.5 × 0.75 in (stops short of front edge).
- Ease bottom front corner of cleats so they read thinner from below.
- Pre-drill screw holes for stud locations.

5. Pre-finish check
-------------------
[ ] Both shelves identical within 1/32 in
[ ] Left end + back edge square and crisp
[ ] Cleat pairs coplanar when dry-clamped in corner
[ ] Shelf sits tight to both walls with no rock
