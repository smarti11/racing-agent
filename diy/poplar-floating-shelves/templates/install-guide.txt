INSTALLATION GUIDE — Left Wall + Back Wall Cleats
=================================================
Prerequisites: wall marks done, shelves and cleats finished/cured, studs mapped
on BOTH walls.

Defaults
--------
Shelf: 20 × 6 × 1.5 in
Clear gap: 12 in
Lower shelf bottom above lid: 26 in
Layout: left end to left wall, back edge to rear wall

1. Install lower cleats
-----------------------
[ ] Mount BACK-WALL cleat level at lower shelf underside elevation; screws into studs
[ ] Mount LEFT-WALL cleat at the SAME elevation; screws into studs
[ ] Check with a long level / winding sticks: both cleat tops coplanar
[ ] Dry-set lower shelf into the corner — tight to both walls, no rock

2. Fasten lower shelf
---------------------
[ ] From above, drive finish screws or pin nails into each cleat (pre-drill)
[ ] Optional: small L-bracket under the inside corner into both walls/studs
[ ] Confirm level front-to-back and left-to-right

3. Install upper shelf
----------------------
[ ] Upper shelf BOTTOM = lower shelf BOTTOM + 1.5 + 12 = lower bottom + 13.5 in
[ ] Repeat cleat install on both walls, then seat and fasten upper shelf
[ ] Match front overhang / right-end position to the lower shelf

4. Final checks
---------------
[ ] Left ends flush/tight to left wall
[ ] Back edges flush/tight to rear wall
[ ] 12 in clear between shelves
[ ] Basket clears lower shelf; right ends clear towel cabinet
[ ] No wobble under hand pressure

Load guidance
-------------
Dual-wall cleats are stronger than single-wall floating rods. Still keep loads
moderate (folded towels, small bottles). Avoid heavy stacks at the unsupported
front-right corner.
