INSTALLATION GUIDE — Centered Floating Shelves
==============================================
Prerequisites: wall marks complete (wall-marking-worksheet.txt), shelves finished and cured,
brackets and structural screws on hand.

Defaults from plan
------------------
Shelf: 20 × 6 × 1.5 in
Clear gap between shelves: 12 in
Lower shelf bottom above tank lid: 26 in (acceptable 24–28)
Center: tank lid centerline

1. Mount lower bracket first
----------------------------
[ ] Align bracket to lower shelf marks; long level on bracket plate/rods
[ ] Drive screws into studs only (use plate/ledger kit if studs miss shelf center)
[ ] Confirm level again after first screws, then finish fastening
[ ] Dry-fit lower shelf — flush to wall, centered (± equal overhang vs tank)

2. Mount upper bracket
----------------------
[ ] Measure from finished TOP of lower shelf (or from lower shelf BOTTOM + 1.5 + 12)
  Upper shelf BOTTOM = lower shelf BOTTOM + 1.5 in + 12 in = lower bottom + 13.5 in
[ ] Level upper bracket; fasten into studs
[ ] Dry-fit upper shelf; match left/right overhang to lower shelf (use centerline tape)

3. Final seat and lock
----------------------
[ ] Slide both shelves fully onto rods/plates
[ ] Tighten kit set screws if provided
[ ] Check: level, no wobble, tight wall gap, clear of towel cabinet, basket clears lower shelf

4. Load guidance
----------------
Stay under bracket rating (plan assumes ≥ 30–50 lb per shelf). Prefer lighter décor,
folded hand towels, and small bottles — not heavy ceramics stacked at the front edge.

Rollback / adjustment
---------------------
If off-center: remove shelves, loosen bracket, shift using centerline tape, re-level, re-drive
into studs (fill abandoned holes with appropriate anchors/plugs if needed before paint touch-up).
