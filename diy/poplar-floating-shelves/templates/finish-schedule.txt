FINISH SCHEDULE — Bathroom Poplar (Corner Shelves)
==================================================
Goal: sealed shelves + finished or wall-matched cleats.

1. Sanding
----------
[ ] 120 → 180 → 220 on all shelf faces
[ ] Keep LEFT END and BACK EDGE crisp (only light de-burr)
[ ] Sand cleats; ease lower visible edges

2. Optional stain
-----------------
[ ] Conditioner + stain if matching other wood; do both shelves together
[ ] Or leave natural poplar under clear coat

3. Topcoat
----------
[ ] Waterborne polyurethane preferred for humidity
[ ] Coat all shelf faces including underside
[ ] Mask or leave undersides of cleat tops bare if gluing/screwing later, OR
    finish fully and rely on mechanical fasteners only
[ ] 2–3 coats with 220 scuff between; extra coat on top face
[ ] Cleats: paint to match wall OR clear-coat with shelves
[ ] Cure 24–48 h beyond dry-to-touch before install

4. Pre-install
--------------
[ ] Matching sheen on both shelves
[ ] Dry-fit in corner still tight after finish film
[ ] Cleat screw holes still clear
