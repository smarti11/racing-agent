POPLAR CORNER SHELVES — CUT LIST
================================
Final shelf size: 20 in L × 6 in D × 1.5 in T — qty 2
Mount: cleats on back wall AND left wall

SHELVES
-------
OPTION A — Laminate from 3/4 in stock
Part                    Qty   Rough cut          Notes
Shelf face (top)        2     20.5 × 6.25 × 3/4  Trim after glue-up
Shelf face (bottom)     2     20.5 × 6.25 × 3/4  Grain parallel
Waste / test scrap      1     ~12 × 6 × 3/4      Fit checks against corner

After glue-up: mill each blank to 20.00 × 6.00 × 1.50 in.
Square the LEFT end carefully — it must sit tight to the left wall.
Square the BACK edge — it must sit tight to the rear wall.
Ease only the front and right edges (leave left end + back edge crisp).

Part                    Qty   Rough cut          Notes
